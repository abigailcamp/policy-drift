title: Reading sanctions like code
date: 2026-05-27
author: Abby Camp
---

Sanctions programs behave a lot like software: small patches can change outcomes dramatically, and the “runtime” is a mixture of statute, executive orders, regulations, and agency guidance.

When you compare versions, look for:

- **Scope**: who and what is covered (definitions, covered activities, jurisdiction).
- **Delegations**: which agencies gain or lose authority.
- **Exceptions**: carve‑outs, licensing pathways, and compliance safe harbors.
- **Timing**: effective dates, wind‑down periods, and reporting requirements.

The point of a redline isn’t to admire formatting—it’s to build a mental model of how the legal system is being recompiled over time.
