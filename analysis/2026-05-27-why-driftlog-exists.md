---
title: Why PolicyDrift exists
date: 2026-05-27
author: Abby Camp
---

Policy changes often arrive as small edits inside long documents—an amended clause, a new definition, a changed carve‑out. Those edits can reshape what sanctions authorities allow, what agencies can enforce, and what the public can verify.

PolicyDrift is a simple attempt to make that drift legible: a version log of primary-source text, plus redline diffs and analyst notes that explain what changed and why it matters.
