About

What PolicyDrift is

PolicyDrift is a research tool for tracking how U.S. federal policy instruments change over time—executive orders, public laws, and related authorities. It keeps a versioned log of source text and makes amendments legible through redline diffs and short analyst notes.

How it works

PolicyDrift collects documents from official government sources, including govinfo.gov, federalregister.gov, and congress.gov. You can also paste official text manually via Upload when an API copy isn’t available or you want a specific cut of the document. Each version is normalized and deduplicated so identical text isn’t re-added.

Versions are compared using text diffing. The tool flags additions and deletions, and it also tries to surface structural changes (like moved sections or reshuffled paragraphs) so you can see what changed without rereading the entire document. Results are for research and education only.
